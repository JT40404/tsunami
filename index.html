How it works

      

        

          phase 1
          
On the curve: build

          
While TSUNAMI trades on the pump.fun bonding curve, buybacks are made with PUMP. Every buy adds PUMP to the curve’s reserves, and when the curve completes, those reserves become the liquidity in TSUNAMI’s PumpSwap pool.

        

        
migration→

        

          phase 2
          
In the pool: burn

          
After TSUNAMI migrates to PumpSwap, buybacks are made from the TSUNAMI/PUMP pool and the tokens are burned. Burned tokens are gone for good, so total supply only goes down.

        

      


      
Why it compounds

      

        

          
Building liquidity and burning supply each help on their own. Done in this order, they build on each other.

          
A deeper pool from day one. Buybacks on the curve add to the reserves TSUNAMI migrates with, so it arrives on PumpSwap with a deeper pool. Large buys and sells move the price less, for every trader who comes after.

          
Fewer tokens. Every burn after migration permanently removes tokens, so that pool backs a smaller and smaller supply.

          
One multiplies the other. A buyback in the pool leaves its PUMP behind in the pool while the tokens it bought are burned. Liquidity per token rises from both sides at once, and because burns can’t be undone, every one keeps counting.

          
That’s the long game: a token whose market gets steadily deeper and more stable over time, instead of one that relies on hype to hold up.
