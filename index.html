      What TSUNAMI is
      
            
      
              
      A pump.fun token built to snowball. Buybacks start on the bonding curve and burn supply. After migration they switch to building liquidity. Every one is on-chain and shows up on this site automatically.
      
              
      Replace this paragraph with your own story: why the token exists, who is behind it and what holders can expect. Keep it plain and specific, people trust what they can check.
      
            
      
          
      
          
        
      

        
      Two phases, one wave
      
        
      
          
      
            phase 1
            
      On the curve
      
            
      While the token is on the pump.fun bonding curve.
      
            
        
              
      1. The buyback wallet buys TSUNAMI on the curve.
        2. 
              
      2. Those tokens are burned.
        3. 
              
      3. Total supply drops for good, and the curve fills toward migration.
        4. 
            
      
          
      
          
      
            phase 2
            
      On PumpSwap
      
            
      After the curve completes and the token migrates.
      
            
        
              
      1. The buyback wallet buys TSUNAMI from the pool.
        2. 
              
      2. The tokens are paired with an equal value of SOL.
        3. 
              
      3. Both go into the pool, so large trades move the price less.
        4. 
            
      
          
      
        
      
        
      Each phase feeds the next: a smaller supply going into migration means the liquidity added afterwards backs fewer tokens.
